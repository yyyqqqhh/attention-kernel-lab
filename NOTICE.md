# Provenance and license status

This project is a new educational implementation of tiled attention and online
softmax. It contains no employer source, internal workloads, production results,
or proprietary performance data. Algorithm references and their authors are
credited in the design documentation; this repository makes no claim to invent
online softmax or FlashAttention.

A distribution license has not been selected. No license grant is made by this
repository. Dependency licenses remain with their respective projects.

The source archive's fixed ZIP timestamps serve reproducible packaging only;
they do not represent development dates. Actual local validation timestamps are
recorded in `results/validation.json`.
